## Core Principles

### 1. Build ONE App First
- Don't spread resources across multiple products
- Nail product-market fit before diversifying

### 2. Distribution First (Not After)
> "90% time on marketing"

The #1 mistake: building a great app with no distribution plan.

**Distribution channels:**
- **21 posts/day** on social (high volume testing)
- **UGC (User-Generated Content)** — authentic content from real users
- **Influencer partnerships** — micro-influencers > macro
- **Paid ads** — Meta, TikTok (test with $500-1K budgets)

### 3. Onboarding is Make-or-Break

**Critical rules:**
- **Story structure** — frame app as a journey, not a tool
- **Aha moment within 1 minute** — user must see value immediately
- **Let user experience core feature** before paywall
- **Show paywall after 10-15 minutes** — not before

> "If they don't feel the value in 60 seconds, they've already churned."

## Actionable Insights for iOS Home Screen Organizer

### Distribution Strategy

| Channel | Recommendation | Priority |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| TikTok | 21 posts/week (not 21/day to start), focus on "before/after" home screen transformations | P1 |
| Influencers | Micro-influencers (5K-50K followers) in productivity/lifestyle niche | P1 |
| UGC | Partner with UGC platforms (Creator.co, Fourthwall) for authentic demo content | P1 |
| Reddit | Product Hunt launch + r/ios, r/homepage subreddits | P2 |
| ASO | Optimize for "organize," "home screen," "widgets" keywords | P2 |

### Onboarding Flow

Based on the "aha moment in 1 minute" rule:

1. **First 15 seconds:** Show value proposition (transform messy → organized home screen)
2. **15-30 seconds:** One-tap screenshot capture demo
3. **30-60 seconds:** Show the "magic" — AI organization preview
4. **After aha moment:** Subtle upgrade prompt (not hard paywall)

### Marketing:Product Time Ratio

| Phase | Time on Marketing | Time on Product |
|-------|------------------|-----------------|
| Pre-launch | 70% | 30% |
| Launch month | 90% | 10% |
| Post-launch (stable) | 60% | 40% |
