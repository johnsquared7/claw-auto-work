# App Store Preview Video Best Practices for Home Screen Organizer
**Research Brief | Oracle | March 5, 2026**

---

## Executive Summary

App Store preview videos can boost conversion rates by **20-40%** compared to screenshots alone. For a utility app like Home Screen Organizer, the video is critical to demonstrate the AI auto-organization magic in action.

---

## Technical Requirements (Apple Guidelines)

| Spec | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Duration | 15-30 seconds |
| Quantity | Up to 3 per localization |
| Format | .mov, .mp4, .m4v (H.264 + AAC) |
| Resolution | Device-specific (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro Max: 2796×1290px) |
| Max Size | 500MB per video |
| Content | **Only** in-app footage recorded on device |
| Audio | Optional (muted autoplay default) |

---

## The 11 Commandments (Key Findings)

### 1. First 5 Seconds = Make or Break
Users decide within 3-5 seconds whether to keep watching. Lead with your app's most compelling feature—the AI auto-organization moment.

### 2. Show, Don't Tell
Visual storytelling > narration. Use colors, graphics, and dynamic sequences. For Home Screen Organizer: show the messy → organized transformation.

### 3. Optimize for Silent Viewing
Videos autoplay muted. Add subtitles or animated text highlights. Visuals must communicate the story alone.

### 4. Brand Early (Seconds 1-2)
Display your app logo within first few seconds. Many users don't watch the full video—early branding ensures recognition.

### 5. Large, Clear UI Elements
Mobile screens are small. Text, buttons, and key UI must be easily readable. Emphasize the most important features.

### 6. Segment Your Video
Create 3 short clips (10s each) rather than one continuous story. This enables:
- A/B testing different sequences
- Platform-specific optimization
- Repurposing for ads

### 7. Localize Strategically
Adjust for different regions—language, cultural norms, feature emphasis. Consider region-specific use cases.

### 8. No Promotional Content
Apple prohibits: pricing, "Download now" CTAs, marketing fluff, simulated scenes, or hands operating device. Only real in-app footage.

### 9. Minimal Text Overlay
Text must be brief, clear, and easily localizable. Avoid walls of text.

### 10. Test and Iterate
A/B test visuals, sequences, and messages. Track engagement and conversion rates.

### 11. Follow Technical Specs Exactly
Wrong dimensions or format = rejection. Test on multiple devices before submission.

## Recommended Video Structure for Home Screen Organizer

### Video 1: The "Aha Moment" (30s)
1. **0-3s**: Brand intro + messy home screen
2. **3-10s**: Tap "Organize" → AI analysis animation
3. **10-20s**: Magic transformation (messy → organized)
4. **20-28s**: Highlight key features (folders, suggestions, widgets)
5. **28-30s**: Logo + tagline (no CTA)

### Video 2: Feature Deep-Dive (15-20s)
Focus on widget customization and folder organization for power users.

### Video 3: Before/After (15-20s)
Quick cuts of multiple organization transformations across different screen styles.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Showing simulated/hands operating device  
❌ Using promotional messaging or pricing  
❌ Too much text overlay  
❌ Starting slow (users bounce in 3s)  
❌ Poor audio mixing (if using sound)  
❌ Ignoring device-specific optimization
