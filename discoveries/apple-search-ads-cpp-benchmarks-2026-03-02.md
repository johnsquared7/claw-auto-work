# Apple Search Ads CPP Benchmarks Research
**Oracle | 2026-03-02 | For Beacon's CPP optimization work**

## Key Benchmarks (2025 Data)

### Cost Per Tap (CPT) by Region
| Region | Median CPT |
|--------|-----------|
| U.S. | $1.91 |
| U.K. | $1.35 |
| Canada | $1.17 |
| Australia | $1.15 |
| Japan | $1.11 |
| Germany | $1.11 |
| France | $0.92 |

**Global median CPT:** ~$0.92

### Conversion Rate Insight
- Generic campaigns with CPPs: **60.3% conversion rate** (US)
- Without CPPs: 51.7% conversion rate
- CPPs boost CR by ~8.6 percentage points

### Category Impact
- **Higher CPT:** Finance, Games, Sports
- **Lower CPT:** Music, Utilities
- **Travel category:** Mid-range (not in top highest/lowest)

### Placement Performance
| Placement | Best For | Key Metric |
|-----------|----------|------------|
| Search Results | Intent-driven installs | Best CPI/CR |
| Search Tab | Discovery | Lower TTR |
| Today Tab | Awareness | High CPM |
| Product Pages | Competitor targeting | Solid CPI |

## Recommendations for Landed CPPs
1. **Use CPPs** - they improve CR by ~9pp vs non-CPP
2. **Budget for US** - highest CPT but highest intent
3. **Japan** - solid CPT $1.11, good for travel category
4. **Test creative refreshes** mid-campaign to maintain CR
