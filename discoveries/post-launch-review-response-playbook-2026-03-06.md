# Post-Launch Review Response Playbook
## Home Screen Organizer App - App Store & Social Media

**Created:** 2026-03-06
**Purpose:** Turn negative feedback into loyalty signals; protect brand reputation during launch window.

---

## Quick Reference: Response Decision Tree

```
INCOMING FEEDBACK
       │
       ├─► APP STORE REVIEW
       │        │
       │        ├─► 1-2 Stars → URGENT: Respond within 4 hours
       │        ├─► 3 Stars → Same day response
       │        ├─► 4 Stars → Within 24 hours (ask what would make it 5)
       │        └─► 5 Stars → Within 48 hours (thank + share feedback channel)
       │
       └─► SOCIAL MEDIA
                │
                ├─► Bug/Technical → Acknowledge + direct to support
                ├─► Feature Request → Thank + note for roadmap
                ├─► Pricing Question → Transparent + value prop
                ├─► Competitor Mention → Stay positive, don't bash
                └─► Praise → Thank + encourage share/review
```

## App Store Review Response Templates

### 🚨 Critical (1-2 Stars) - Bug/Crash

**Template:**
> "Hi [Name], we're really sorry this happened. That's not the experience we want for you. Could you email us at support@homescreenorganizer.app with your device model and iOS version? We'll investigate immediately and get back to you within 24 hours."

**Key elements:** Empathy first → Clear action → Specific next step → Response time promise

---

### ⚠️ Negative (1-2 Stars) - Feature Missing

**Template:**
> "Thanks for the honest feedback, [Name]. We hear you on [specific feature]. It's on our roadmap for [timeframe/under consideration]. In the meantime, [workaround if available]. We'd love to earn a higher rating from you."

**Key elements:** Acknowledge → Explain status → Offer alternative → Open door for return

---

### 😐 Mixed (3 Stars) - "It's okay but..."

**Template:**
> "Appreciate you taking the time, [Name]. What would make this a 5-star app for you? We're actively working on improvements and your input helps prioritize. Drop us a line at feedback@homescreenorganizer.app if you want to chat more."

**Key elements:** Thank → Ask directly → Show active development → Provide channel

---

### 👍 Positive (4-5 Stars)

**Template:**
> "Thank you so much, [Name]! 🙌 Glad [specific thing they mentioned] is working well for you. We're always adding new features—let us know if there's anything you'd love to see next!"

**Key elements:** Genuine thanks → Reference their words → Future-focused → Invitation

## Social Media Response Templates

### Twitter/X Public Response

**Bug report:**
> "Sorry to hear that! 😔 Could you DM us your iOS version and device? We'll get it sorted."

**Feature request:**
> "Great idea! Added to our feature tracker. Stay tuned for updates. 🚀"

**Praise:**
> "Made our day! 🧡 If you haven't already, a quick App Store review helps other folks find us!"

---

### Reddit Response Guidelines

**DO:**
- Be transparent about what you're working on
- Share your developer journey honestly
- Respond to criticism with humility, not defensiveness
- Update threads when issues are fixed

**DON'T:**
- Argue with users publicly
- Make promises you can't keep
- Hide that you're the developer (disclose early)
- Delete negative comments (looks shady)

**Template for r/ios launch post criticism:**
> "Fair point on [specific issue]. We're a small team and this is our first launch. Here's what we're doing to address it: [action]. Happy to answer any other questions."

---

### TikTok/Instagram Comments

**Negative:**
> "Dang, sorry it didn't work for you! 😔 What happened? DM us and we'll help troubleshoot."

**Feature request:**
> "Noted! 🔥 Adding to the list. What else would you want to see?"

**Pricing question:**
> "Free to try the core features! Premium unlocks [X, Y, Z] if you find it useful. No pressure!"

## Negative Review Recovery Strategy

### The 4-Step Recovery Play

1. **Acknowledge fast** - Within 4 hours for 1-2 star reviews during launch week
2. **Take it offline** - Move to email/DM for detailed troubleshooting
3. **Fix and follow up** - Email them when the fix ships
4. **Request update** - Ask them to update their review if satisfied

### When Users Update Reviews

**They will update if:**
- Their specific issue was addressed
- You followed up personally
- The fix shipped quickly

**Follow-up email template:**
> "Hi [Name],
>
> Quick update: We just shipped version [X.Y] which fixes the [specific issue] you reported. Wanted to let you know personally since you took the time to tell us about it.
>
> If it's working better now, we'd be grateful if you'd consider updating your App Store review. No pressure either way—your feedback already helped us improve.
>
> Thanks for helping make Home Screen Organizer better!"

## Red Flags (Escalate to John)

- Security/privacy concerns in reviews
- Multiple reviews mentioning same crash
- Influencer/media negative mention
- Review bombing pattern (5+ 1-stars in 1 hour)
- Potential App Store guideline violation mentioned
