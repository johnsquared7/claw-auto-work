# Wave 2 Research: Channel Expansion
**Oracle | March 4, 2026**

## Research Objective
Identify new user acquisition channels for Landed app beyond existing Reddit/wave-1 tactics.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. AI-Powered User Acquisition
- Apps using AI-driven UA see **143% higher user growth** vs traditional approaches
- Better retention and LTV due to intent-based targeting
- **Recommendation**: Test AI-optimized ad platforms (Meta Advantage+, Google PMax)

### 2. OEM Partnerships (Mobile Pre-install/Bundling)
- Diversify beyond app store dependency
- AVOW and similar partners connect with device manufacturers
- Bypasses crowded app store search
- **Recommendation**: Low priority for v1 — requires scale

### 3. Influencer Marketing (TikTok/Instagram)
- Borrowed trust model > algorithm-dependent channels
- 3-6 month sustained partnerships outperform one-offs
- Micro-influencers (10K-100K) better ROI for niche apps
- **Recommendation**: P1 — Travel/niche creators, test with $2-5K pilot

### 4. Podcast Advertising
- Growing channel for thought leadership + app discovery
- Host-read ads in travel/tech podcasts
- **Recommendation**: P2 — Identify 5-10 relevant shows, benchmark CPMs

### 5. Alternative App Stores
- Huawei AppGallery, Samsung Galaxy Store
- Higher penetration in Asia, LATAM, emerging markets
- **Recommendation**: P3 — Future international expansion

### 6. Content/SEO (Owned Channel)
- Blog content targeting travel organization keywords
- Inbound from web → app install
- **Recommendation**: P1 — Low cost, aligns with wiki website

### 7. Referral & Viral Loops
- In-app referral incentives
- Share-to-unlock features
- **Recommendation**: P2 — Feature requirement for v2

---

## Prioritized Channel Roadmap

| Priority | Channel | Est. Cost | Effort | Notes |
|----------|---------|-----------|--------|-------|
| P1 | Content/SEO | $ | Medium | Leverage existing wiki traffic |
| P1 | TikTok Influencers | $2-5K | Low | Pilot test |
| P2 | Podcast Ads | $5-10K | Medium | Travel/tech shows |
| P2 | Referral Loop | Dev | High | Requires v2 dev |
| P3 | OEM Partnerships | $$ | High | Scale phase |
| P3 | Alt Stores | $$ | Medium | International |

---

## Key Insight
**Borrowed trust** (influencers, referrals, partnerships) is more resilient to algorithm shifts than paid media alone. Prioritize relationship-based channels over pure performance ads for long-term growth.
