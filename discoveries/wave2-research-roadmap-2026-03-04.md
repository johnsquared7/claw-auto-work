# Wave 2 Research Roadmap
**Oracle | March 4, 2026**

## Priority Research Backlog for Wave 2

### Top Priority (P1) — Launch-Blocking or High-Impact

**1. User Feedback Loops & In-App Feedback Mechanisms**
- How to collect actionable feedback without hurting UX
- NPS vs. contextual surveys vs. community channels
- Implement in Wave 2: feedback widget + review prompts
- *Deliverable: 1-page implementation brief*

**2. Retention Cohort Analysis Framework**
- Define cohort segments (by source, geography, usage pattern)
- Build retention curves (D1/D7/D30) for Wave 1 users
- Identify drop-off points and correlates
- *Deliverable: cohort segmentation schema + SQL queries*

**3. Competitive Landscape Updates**
- Monitor new entrants in home screen organizer space
- Track feature parity with Android launchers
- Watch for AI-powered organization features
- *Deliverable: updated competitive matrix (quarterly)*

---

### Medium Priority (P2) — Strategic Enablers

**4. Channel Expansion Research**
- Product Hunt launch viability and timing
- alternative to Reddit r/ios (r/productivity, r/iphone)
- Influencer outreach (YouTube, TikTok)
- *Deliverable: channel prioritization matrix*

**5. Technical Feasibility: Advanced Features**
- Home screen snapshot analysis (privacy implications)
- AI-driven folder suggestions (on-device vs cloud)
- Widget customization APIs in iOS 19
- *Deliverable: technical feasibility briefs*

**6. Monetization Deep-Dive**
- Freemium model validation post-launch
- Price sensitivity testing
- A/B test results from similar apps
- *Deliverable: monetization strategy brief*

### Lower Priority (P3) — Future-Proofing

- *Deliverable: technical assessment*
